## 🇬🇧 English

An epic cinematic lighting designer that reads a storyboard, plot or scene and auto-selects a lighting setup, then outputs precise light descriptions you can paste straight into an AI image or video prompt — so every frame has designed light rather than default even lighting. Tier 1 covers three-point lighting variants, emotional lighting-ratio mapping, eight light directions, time-of-day light and low-key/high-key; Tier 2 goes advanced with volumetric light, chiaroscuro, negative fill, colour-temperature contrast, gobo texture projection and practical-light storytelling. A built-in story-to-lighting recipe lookup lets you pull a recipe directly from plot mood and scene, and one lighting description serves both image and video use. It guards its boundaries: colour grading/LUTs, 3D render-engine light parameters, stage DMX control and pure colour theory are out of scope, and it points you to the right tool instead.

### ✨ What it does

- Storyboard, plot or scene in → an auto-selected lighting setup out, described precisely enough to paste into an AI image or video prompt
- Tier 1 basics: three-point lighting and its variants, emotional lighting-ratio mapping, eight light directions, time-of-day light, low-key/high-key
- Tier 2 advanced: volumetric light, chiaroscuro, negative fill, colour-temperature contrast, gobo texture projection, practical-light storytelling
- Built-in story-to-lighting recipe lookup pulls a ready recipe from plot mood and scene instead of assembling descriptors from scratch
- One lighting description serves both images and video, with per-medium adjustments for each generation path
- Boundary guard sends you elsewhere for grading/LUTs, 3D render-engine light parameters, stage DMX control and pure colour theory
